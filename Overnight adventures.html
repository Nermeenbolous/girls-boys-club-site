Overnight Adventures Program

Designed to help Club members (age 14-18) stretch their wings and explore the greater community[cite: 35]. Members can explore museums, visit the beach, or hike National Forest trails[cite: 36].

Each trip leaves Friday afternoon and returns Sunday evening[cite: 37]. While trips require a nominal fee, no teen will be turned away for an inability to pay[cite: 37, 38].

Club members may exchange 10 hours of work in the Club facility to earn credits to defray the costs[cite: 39].
